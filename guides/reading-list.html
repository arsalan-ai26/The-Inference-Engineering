Guide

Reading list

Every research paper the book cites, in one place: 108 papers, grouped by the part that first uses them and ordered as a reader should meet them. Each has one line on why to read it (from the chapter that cites it), the lab it comes from, and a tier. Core (40 papers) are the ones a practitioner should have read; extended are for depth on one topic. 37 come from Chinese labs and universities, whose inference work is now some of the most detailed published.

How to read the Chinese-lab technical reports

Model reports from DeepSeek, Moonshot, Alibaba, MiniMax and others are long (often 50 pages or more) and mostly about training data and benchmarks. The inference content is usually in one section: DeepSeek-V3, section 3.4 "Inference and Deployment"; DeepSeek-V4, section 3.5 "Inference Framework" (KV layout, on-disk cache) and 3.1 and 3.3 (expert overlap, batch-invariant kernels); DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash, section 3.2 "Inference System" (persistent KV, bounded replay) and 2.4 (DSpark, FP4 KV); MiniMax-01, section 3.3 "Lightning Attention Inference Optimization"; Insights into DeepSeek-V3, section 2.3 "Increasing Inference Speed". Systems papers (Step-3, MegaScale-Infer, CloudMatrix384) are about inference throughout. Read the abstract, the architecture section and the inference section first, then the evaluation tables for the numbers you need.

Lab tags give the main affiliation listed on each paper, to help you find a group's other work; "see paper" marks ones not confirmed here. The list is generated from the chapters' reference tables by tools/gen_reading_list.py.
